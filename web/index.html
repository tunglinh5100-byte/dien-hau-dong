Experience Vietnamese Mother Goddess worship

Đạo Mẫu is the living faith of the Mother Goddesses of the Three Realms. Choose a spirit, take your seat on the temple mat and follow a hầu đồng ritual from invocation to farewell.

How it works

  1. Choose a spiritEach ritual embodies one spirit of the pantheon, with its own robe, music and dances.
  2. Take a seatSign in to sit on the mat as a devotee and watch the medium at the altar.
  3. Join the ritualBow, clap and choose your robe colour as the ceremony unfolds step by step.
